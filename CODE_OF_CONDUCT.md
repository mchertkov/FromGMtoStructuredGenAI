# Code of Conduct

This is an academic research and teaching repository. Contributors are expected to interact professionally and constructively.

- Critique mathematical claims, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Treat counterexamples and negative results as useful contributions.
- Do not disclose private student information or graded course material without permission.
- Do not post credentials, private datasets, or material you do not have the right to redistribute.
- Give appropriate attribution for substantial intellectual or computational contributions.

Harassing, discriminatory, or personally abusive behavior is not acceptable. Repository maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a productive scholarly environment.
