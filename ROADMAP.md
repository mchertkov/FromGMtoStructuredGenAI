# Roadmap

This roadmap describes the intended evolution of the living book/repository during Fall 2026. It is not a promise that every item will be completed on a fixed date.

## Current foundation

The repository currently contains 56 notebooks supporting the classical graphical-model core, applications, specialized inference/learning chapters, early neuralization material, and the first space–time/compositional generative-model laboratory.

## Fall 2026 priorities

### 1. Consolidate the classical computational spine

- keep exact examples as reference laboratories;
- strengthen success/crossover/failure comparisons for approximate methods;
- add certificates or exact comparisons where feasible;
- improve cross-chapter reuse of Ising/LDPC and Gaussian/Gaussian-mixture benchmark spines.

### 2. Graphical-model neuralization

- learned factors and energies;
- unfolded/learned messages and operators;
- warm-start-then-correct schemes;
- learned references and residual corrections;
- latent-variable, variational, and amortized inference;
- controlled experiments showing when neuralization helps and when it does not.

### 3. Generative-AI structuralization

- space–time factor graphs as the common dynamic object;
- structured transport, scores, and diffusion;
- diffusion smoothing as coarse-graining/nonlocal effective interaction;
- bridges, Doob transforms, path-integral and Feynman–Kac generation;
- PoE / PoIE / PoPE distinctions and exactly solvable benchmarks;
- discrete diffusion, structured autoregression, and Sampling Decisions;
- scientific constraints and reference–correction mechanisms.

### 4. Course-driven additions

MATH 577 will be used to test:

- exposition and notation;
- exercises, problems, and research challenges;
- notebook reproducibility;
- approximation stress tests;
- student-proposed scientific/engineering applications.

### 5. Release discipline

Use date-based tags/releases, for example:

- `2026.08.19` — initial Fall 2026 public snapshot;
- `2026.09.xx` — first course update;
- `2026.10.xx` — neuralization update;
- `2026.12.31` — target substantial year-end manuscript draft.
